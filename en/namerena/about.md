## Using game content

You may use the game’s built-in icons and smileys, generated badges, and battle logs for noncommercial purposes with attribution to Namerena. Commercial use requires the author’s permission, except for embeds that follow the terms below.

## Third-party embeds {#third-party-embeds}

You may [embed the game in an iframe](api.html) for commercial use free of charge if you follow these terms.

- Keep the game’s files and algorithms intact.
- Keep generated content as it appears in the game. You may change the wording through a [custom language pack](custom_lan.html).
- Keep the game title and ads visible and unaltered.
- Use generated content within the game iframe.
