Names ending in `@!` can call up special fighters or set the random seed.

## Challenge a boss

Pick a boss from the challenge menu, or enter its name on a separate team. Try teaming up two of your strongest fighters against one boss. You might need both of them.

## Random seeds

Put a seed such as `seed:123@!` on a line of its own. The seed controls the battle’s random rolls. Reuse it to replay a result, or change it to see what happens with the same lineup.
