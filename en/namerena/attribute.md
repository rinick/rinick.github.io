title: Attributes

Your name and clan determine your base stats. Gear, teammates, and battle effects can change them during play.

| Stat | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **ATK** (Attack) | Raises the damage and accuracy of physical attacks, including physical skills. |
| **DEF** (Defense) | Reduces physical damage taken and helps you dodge physical attacks. |
| **SPD** (Speed) | Determines how often you act. Higher speed usually means a shorter wait until your next turn. |
| **AGI** (Agility) | Affects accuracy and evasion for both physical and magical attacks. Some skills use their own rules. |
| **MAG** (Magic) | Raises the damage and accuracy of magical attacks and improves some spells, including healing. |
| **RES** (Resistance) | Reduces magical damage taken and helps you dodge magical attacks. |
| **INT** (Intelligence) | Affects decisions, target selection, and mana recovery. A basic attack can use magic if MAG is higher than ATK and the fighter has enough mana. |
| **HP** (Hit points) | Damage takes away HP and healing restores it. A fighter is knocked out when their HP reaches zero. |
