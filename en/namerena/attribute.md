Your name and clan determine your base stats. Gear, teammates, and battle effects can change them during play.

## Physical and magical attacks

### Attack (ATK)

Raises the damage and accuracy of physical attacks, including physical skills.

### Defense (DEF)

Reduces physical damage taken and helps you dodge physical attacks.

### Magic (MAG)

Raises the damage and accuracy of magical attacks and improves some spells, including healing.

### Resistance (RES)

Reduces magical damage taken and helps you dodge magical attacks.

## Timing and accuracy

### Speed (SPD)

Determines how often you act. Higher speed usually means a shorter wait until your next turn.

### Agility (AGI)

Affects accuracy and evasion for both physical and magical attacks. Some skills use their own rules.

## Decisions and survival

### Intelligence (INT)

Affects decisions, target selection, and mana recovery. A basic attack can use magic if MAG is higher than ATK and the fighter has enough mana.

### Hit points (HP)

Damage takes away HP and healing restores it. A fighter is knocked out when their HP reaches zero.
