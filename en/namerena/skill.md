A fighter’s name and clan determine their starting skills and proficiency. Higher proficiency makes a skill more likely to activate. Some skills also need enough mana or a suitable target.

## Attack skills

Attack skills are used on a fighter’s turn. The percentages are attack multipliers. Defense, dodges, and status effects determine how much HP the target actually loses.

| Skill | Type | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-hit | Physical | Attacks two or three times, starting at 75% power. Each follow-up hit on the same target loses 15 percentage points. |
| Critical Strike | Physical | Rolls an attack at 115%, 120%, and 125% power and uses the highest roll. |
| Fireball | Magic | Starts at 150% power. Each stack of fire on the target adds 50 percentage points to later Fireballs. |
| Freeze | Magic | Attacks at 70% power and can freeze the target, preventing it from acting for a while. |
| Lightning Strike | Magic | Strikes the same target three to six times at 36% power per hit. The strikes stop if the target dodges. |
| Earthquake | Magic | Hits up to five enemies. Each hit gets weaker as the number of targets increases. |
| Life Drain | Magic | Attacks at 130% power and restores HP equal to roughly half the damage dealt, up to the caster’s maximum HP. |
| Berserk | Magic | Attacks at 100% power. A hit sends the target into a rage on its next turn, when it can lash out at friend or foe. |
| Curse | Magic | Attacks at 100% power. A cursed target sometimes takes double damage from later attacks. |
| Dispel | Magic | Attacks at 100% power and removes beneficial effects on a hit. It deals double damage to familiars. |
| Poison | Magic | Attacks at 100% power. A poisoned fighter loses HP after acting. |
| Sneak and Backstab | Magic | Sneaks on the first turn and backstabs at 400% power on the next. Taking damage in between interrupts the attack. |
| Plague | Magic | Removes a share of the target’s current HP, up to 99%. The caster’s MAG and the target’s RES affect the percentage. |
| Wheel of Life | Magic | Swaps current HP with the target. The caster can gain HP only up to their own maximum. |

## Support skills

| Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Focus | The first use usually raises attack power to 170%. Charging up strengthens the effect. |
| Charge | Triples attack power through the end of the fighter’s next action and strengthens some skills. |
| Charm | Temporarily makes the target treat the caster’s team as allies. |
| Haste | Doubles speed for three turns. |
| Slow | Halves the target’s speed for two turns. |
| Iron Guard | Reduces incoming damage for a short time. The guard can break early. |
| Heal | Restores HP to the caster or a teammate and clears harmful status effects. |
| Revive | Brings back a knocked-out teammate. |
| Blood Sacrifice | Summons a Familiar. The caster also takes half the damage the Familiar receives. |
| Illusion | Summons a Shadow with low HP. It normally takes a while to act, but charging speeds it up. |
| Clone | Creates a copy. Stats drop after cloning, while skills stay the same. |

## Passive skills

| Skill | When it can trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Counter | After taking damage | Answers with a physical attack at the end of the turn. |
| Defend | When taking damage | Halves the damage. |
| Reflect | When attacked | Stops the original attack and counters it with magic. |
| Protect | When a teammate is attacked | Takes the hit in the teammate’s place and halves the damage. |
| Amulet | On a fatal blow | Brings the fighter back with a little HP. |
| Last Stand | After taking damage at low HP | Boosts several combat stats. |
| Shield | On the fighter’s turn | Replenishes a shield that can absorb damage. |
| Raise the Dead | After knocking out an enemy | Raises the defeated fighter as a Zombie that fights for you. |
| Absorb | After knocking out an enemy | Improves the fighter’s stats and grants the defeated enemy’s skills. |
